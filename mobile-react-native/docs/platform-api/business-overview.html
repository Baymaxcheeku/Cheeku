Grouped by what a partner's users do with them: talk to Cheeku, book care, understand their health, and the plumbing that runs it.

Conversations

07 · Data isolation

Four walls around every piece of data

The checks run where the data lives, on every read and every write, with a default of deny. A read permission can never delete anything, and partners can't delete a person's records at all.

PartnerOne partner never sees another partner's data, users or traffic. Sandbox and production are separate.
FamilyA grant is bound to one account and never reaches another family.
Family memberOnly the members on the grant, even within the same family.
RecordEvery record carries its source
HbA1c from a lab reportsource: lab report
Blood pressure entered by the personsource: the person
Steps sent by Partner Bsource: Partner B
A partner reads only what it created unless the person ticks history. Partner-sent reports wait for the person's check and never replace their own data.
Partners can't link people across partners
Each partner sees a family member under an id made only for that partner.
Same person
Partner A sees
mem_7QX2…
Partner B sees
mem_K9PL…
The two ids can't be matched, and no partner sees Cheeku's internal ids.
Deny by default
Anything not explicitly opened to partners stays closed. Insurance policies, locations, notifications and consent records are not offered in version 1.
Permissions can't escalate
A read permission can never delete anything, and partners can't delete a person's records at all.

08 · Conversations

Safety comes first on every answer

Cheeku checks for anything urgent before any answer is written. Emergencies get fixed, tested guidance; everything else is answered from the person's own records.

Never a clinical decision
No diagnosis, no prescription, no dose changes.
Partners send questions, not context
Cheeku builds the context from what the person granted. Supplied history, urgency or instructions are refused.
The person sees it too
Chats held through a partner appear in their Cheeku app under the partner's name. Answers can stream as they are written.

11 · Events

Events say what changed, never the health details

The partner fetches details with its own access, which Cheeku checks again at that moment. A revoked grant gets nothing, even from a late event.

12 · Security and compliance

Deny by default, checked on every request, recorded without content

Open standards for access, encryption everywhere, data kept in India, and an agreement with every partner.

Access

OAuth 2.1 with PKCE
Standard sign-in; partners never see Cheeku credentials.
Signed app identity
Apps sign with a private key; no shared secrets. Mutual TLS available.
Short-lived tokens
15-minute access, checked against the grant on every request.
Deny by default
Scope and family-member checks on every read and write.

Protection

Strict validation
Published schema, unknown fields refused, 256 KB request cap.
Encryption
TLS 1.2+ in transit, AES-256 at rest, secrets in a managed vault.
Audit without content
Who, which grant, which endpoint, result and timing. People can see it.
Abuse protection
Rate limits; a sudden bulk read suspends the grant and alerts on-call.

Assurance

Independent testing
Penetration test before the first live partner, then yearly.
Data in India
Personal and health data is stored and processed in India.
Law and regulation
DPDP Act 2023 · CERT-In 6-hour reporting · RBI payment rules · prescription rules.
Partner agreements
Purpose, retention, delete on revoke, no sale, no re-identification, no training.
