Case study · 1 Nov 2026 · founders only · companion to the Jivi teardown

AQ did not win on AI. It won on distribution, trust and fulfilment.

Ant Group's AI health app went from launch in June 2025 to 150 million users in September 2026. Jivi, with a better benchmark score, shut down in June 2026. This page breaks down why, what is still unproven about AQ, and which parts of it a ₹26 crore Indian startup can actually use.

Evidence levels: Verified company release or named source Reported press or analyst claim Analysis our inference

The verdict in five lines

  1. AQ's success is scale, not yet a business: 150M users and about 20M questions a day, with no disclosed revenue model. Ant's CEO calls it a "must-win" with saturated spending through 2026.
  2. It worked because Ant brought a decade of hospital rails, 300,000 verified doctors, insurance payment and a billion Alipay users to a product that answers, then books, then pays. The model was the least special part.
  3. Jivi had the model and none of the rest. Cheeku sits in between: real partner rails and a sharper user (the family health manager), but no super-app, no doctor network of its own and a tiny budget.
  4. What transfers to India: no-ads trust, named doctors who review AI answers, built-for-parents design, fulfilment in the same chat, and habit campaigns. What does not: Alipay, the Spring Festival Gala, cash red packets and buying a doctor platform.
  5. The better way for Cheeku: WhatsApp for reach, the app for fulfilment, employers for revenue, named doctors for trust, and one free hero campaign instead of paid installs.

Breakdown

Seven reasons AQ worked

Ranked by how much each would be missed if it were taken away. The first three are things money alone cannot buy quickly.

1 · A decade of rails before the app Verified

Alipay has booked hospital appointments since 2014. AQ launched already connected to 5,000 hospitals, with medicine purchase and payment through basic medical insurance e-certificates. An industry founder told 健闻咨询 the core value of a health app is fulfilment, not information. AQ could fulfil on day one.

2 · It bought the doctors' trust Verified

Ant acquired Haodf (Jan 2025), now AQ for Doctor with 300,000 verified physicians. 500+ named physicians run AI doctor agents trained on their own practice. An "AI answer plus doctor review" option covers 16 specialties. The AI is never alone.

3 · It said what it is not Verified

"AQ does not provide medical diagnoses or replace professional doctors." On 29 Dec 2025 it published a pledge: no ads, no paid ranking, no commercial influence in answers. It renamed itself from AQ to A-Fu ("good fortune"), from a tool to a health friend.

4 · Distribution and money at national scale Reported

A billion Alipay users, the CCTV Spring Festival Gala, ¥16.8 red packets for new users, CCTV partnership, and an ad spend of "a few hundred million yuan" in the rename month. CEO Han Xinyi: a must-win, with saturated investment in 2026.

5 · Built for parents Reported

55% of users in tier-3 cities and below, a third middle-aged or older. Elder mode with large type, voice questions, and recognition of 10+ dialects including Cantonese and Hokkien. The family is the unit even when one person types.

6 · Habit campaigns, not features Verified

"Free report reading for 100 million people" with CCTV; a national weight-loss drive with a ¥0.01 body-fat scale and Huawei, Xiaomi device links. 2.75M people joined and lost 2.5M kg by Sep 2026.

7 · Government as a partner Verified

Digital health assistants with the Zhejiang and Chongqing health commissions, a medical-insurance assistant (Yibaoer) with local insurance bureaus, and its Healthcare Large Model inside hospital AI appliances.

What was not a reason

Model quality. Ant has a Healthcare Large Model, but no independent safety evaluation of AQ has been published (UCL review, Sep 2026). AQ's edge was everything around the model, which is exactly where Jivi had nothing.

Honest cracks

What is still unproven about AQ

Retention after the cash

QuestMobile (as reported by 证券之星): Q2 2026 monthly downloads down 63.5% and monthly active users down 31.8% on Q1. Analysts claimed over 70M early users were one-time subsidy visitors. Reported

No business model yet

China Electronics News (Jan 2026): the goal is serving and accumulating users; the business model is not yet clear. A user wrote that they wish it had a paid tier. Reported

Concentration risk

One company controls triage, doctor referral, providers, pharmacy and insurance payment, which a UCL review flags as the sharpest conflict-of-interest risk among consumer health AIs. Verified

Read AQ as a strategic loss leader for a company that already earns from payments and insurance. It proves the demand and the product shape. It does not prove that a standalone company can pay for it.

Cheeku's odds

Where Cheeku is stronger than AQ, and where it never will be

Stronger than AQ

  • A paying user. AQ serves parents for free. Cheeku sells to the adult child who already pays for the parents' tests.
  • Neutrality. AQ's own triage routes to its own doctors, pharmacy and payments. Cheeku books the best partner for the pincode and price.
  • The family file. AQ is built around one user with a family section. Cheeku's memory is per member and per family from the start.
  • India has no AQ. No Indian super-app is building this as a standalone, funded product today.

Never as strong as AQ

  • Distribution. No billion-user parent app; every user has to be earned.
  • Capital. AQ spent more on ads in one month than our whole seed. We cannot win a subsidy war, so we must not start one.
  • Payments and insurance. No equivalent of paying hospital bills with insurance inside the app.
  • The threat. If Jio, PhonePe, Paytm or Google build an Indian AQ, they start where Ant started. Our defence is employers, the family graph and speed.

The better way

Eight moves for Cheeku, learned from AQ and Jivi together

Sell to the child, design for the parent

Parent mode, Hindi voice and family sharing on day one; the adult child sets it up and pays.

From AQ: 55% tier-3 users, elder mode, dialects

Every answer ends in an action

A test, a consult, a reminder, a retest. Information is free; fulfilment is the product.

From AQ: fulfilment first. From Jivi: free answers alone die.

Borrow trust from named doctors

A panel of Indian specialists who review AI answers on request. Make the review the paid upgrade.

From AQ: "AI answer plus doctor review" in 16 specialties

Publish a no-ads pledge

No ads, no paid ranking, no partner preference in answers. Put it on the home screen.

From AQ: the 29 Dec 2025 statement

One free hero campaign, not paid installs

"Free report decode" with a lab and a media partner, then a 90-day HbA1c challenge that books two tests per family.

From AQ: report reading for 100M, the weight-loss drive

WhatsApp for reach, the app for fulfilment

Questions and reports arrive on WhatsApp; bookings, the family file and payments live in the app.

From China: rivals without a super-app used WeChat bots

Employers are our Alipay

An employer brings hundreds of families at once, already paid for. Start in month one.

From our model: seat payback about 2.7 months

Never buy users with cash

Red packets bought AQ installs that left (reported). We would buy nothing but churn. Judge every channel on day-30 retention.
