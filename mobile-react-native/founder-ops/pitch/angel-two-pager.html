The problem

Every doctor sees a snapshot. Nobody sees the story.

Chronic disease builds for years in numbers nobody connects: an HbA1c creeping up across three labs, a new prescription clashing with an old one, a follow-up test never booked. A two-minute consult cannot read that history, so the family manages it alone on WhatsApp.

The product

A health manager for the one who looks after everyone

In every Indian home, one person manages the whole family's health. Cheeku gives them one companion that remembers, checks and books. Clinician-signed rules decide; the AI explains; it never diagnoses.

RemembersEvery report, prescription and reading, per family member.
ChecksEach prescription against 10 published standards.
BooksHome labs, doctors, physio across 12,000+ pincodes.
Keeps you on trackDaily nudges, weekly review, quarterly retest.
Proof it works

China built this. It reached 150 million people in 15 months.

Ant Group's AQ answers about 20 million health questions a day. India has no AQ yet, and India's version needs family memory and booking at its core. That is Cheeku.

Why we win

Every day, the family file gets harder to leave

Each report, medicine and booking deepens a family's memory and powers a longevity engine personalised to your lifestyle: what you eat, how you sleep, when to retest. That consented, longitudinal data trains India's own health model and becomes the risk layer insurers have never had.
