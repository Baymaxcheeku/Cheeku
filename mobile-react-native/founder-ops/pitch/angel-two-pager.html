The problem

Health is lost between doctor visits

A consult lasts two minutes. Then families manage everything alone on WhatsApp: reports from three labs, prescriptions from three doctors, and nobody sees the trend.

The product

One companion that remembers, checks, books

Every answer is about your father, not an average man. Clinician-signed rules decide; the AI explains; it never diagnoses.

Proof it works

China built this. It reached 150 million people in 15 months.

Ant Group's AQ answers about 20 million health questions a day. India has no AQ yet, and India's version needs family memory and booking at its core. That is Cheeku.

How we make money

Answers are free. Care is the business.

Families pay for the care they already buy: tests, consults, plans. Employers pay for their staff's families. Insurers come next.
