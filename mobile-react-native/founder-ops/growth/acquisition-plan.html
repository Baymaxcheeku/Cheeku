CheekuCustomer acquisition plan · 2 / 4
Who we go after

One person runs the family's health. We meet them on a bad day.

Front door · everyone

The sick-day searcher

Fever, cold, acidity at 10 pm. Today they ask the chemist or search online. Highest frequency, lowest trust in what is on offer.

"A licensed doctor for everyday illness. ₹49, about 20 minutes."

Wedge 1

Expecting and new parents

About 80 lakh urban births a year. Daily questions, high spend, a new report every month.

"Week 20, sudden pain, next appointment Tuesday. Ask Cheeku now."

Wedge 2

Caregivers of parents

Diabetes, BP, heart. One adult child manages three doctors' prescriptions over WhatsApp.

"Send Papa's prescription. 10 checks, in a minute, free."

Wedge 3

Women's health

Periods, PCOS, thyroid. Reports scattered across clinics, questions they would rather not ask in person.

"Every report in one file. Read in plain Hindi or English."

Launch cities follow doctor capacity: the ₹49 hook runs only where Cheeku Doctor is live (one pilot city from February, more from April). Everywhere else the lead hook is the free report decode and the prescription check.

January to March · the agency sprint

Rent the paid engine. Own the data.

What the agency runs

  • Meta and Google app campaigns, Android first
  • 40 to 60 new creatives a month, Hindi and English
  • Weekly creative and audience tests
  • Fee inside the performance line, about 10% of managed spend

What stays with us

  • Ad accounts, pixels and the attribution tool are ours from day one
  • Every script is cleared by our clinical lead
  • Activation is the target, not installs: paid on cost per active user
  • 90-day contract, weekly review, exit at 30 days' notice

What we learn by March

  • Real cost per install and per active user, by city and language
  • The three hooks that work, out of about 150 tested
  • Which ICP activates and stays
  • Whether the ₹49 doctor hook beats the free report decode

December's ₹20 lakh pays for the waitlist, the first 30 creators' content bank, store listing work, agency onboarding and about ₹5 lakh of test spend, so January starts with tested creatives.

Loops that cost nothing

3.8 lakh users in 2027 arrive without an ad.

Family invite

The Family plan needs the family. Adding a parent or partner sends an invite; each family brings two to four people.

Share cards

A decoded report, a prescription check score, a doctor's prescription: each ends in a card built to forward on WhatsApp.

Referral credit

Give a free doctor review, get one. Costs us about ₹40 when used, against ₹120 for a paid user.

The model counts word of mouth as 14% of last month's users joining each month in 2027. The referral line (5% to 15% of spend) pays for the credits.

Gates

We check weekly. We move money when a number misses.

Week 4
Install to active at 30% or better; cost per active user under ₹150
If not: pause the bottom half of creatives, move the lead hook to whichever of doctor or report decode activates better.
End of March
Blended paid CAC at ₹120; 100 active creators; day-30 retention at 30%
If CAC is over ₹150 for three weeks: cut performance by 20% and move it to the hero campaign and referral credits.
End of June
Creators bring a third of paid users at ₹110 or less; agency contract reviewed
If creators cost more than ads: keep the top 20%, stop the rest, hold the agency another quarter.
End of September
Word of mouth matches paid, month for month; first 15 employers signed
If word of mouth lags: double the referral credit and test a free first doctor review for invited family.

Team and guardrails

Who runs it

  • Athar owns growth until the growth lead joins in the launch quarter
  • Agency runs paid from December to at least June
  • Creator lead second hire; content and community lead third
  • Performance marketer joins in 2028 and brings paid in-house
  • Deepesh runs employers, benefits platforms and pharmacy partners
  • Growth team: 3 people by December 2027, 5 by June 2028, as in the model

What we will not do

  • No cure or guarantee claims; no before and after
  • Doctors explain, they do not endorse; qualifications shown on every post
  • No ₹49 doctor ads in a city before the doctors are live there
  • No antibiotics or controlled drugs, so no ads that imply them
  • Paid partnerships labelled; health data never used for ad targeting
  • A written legal opinion on advertising a doctor review before the first doctor ad runs
