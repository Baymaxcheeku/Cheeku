What this assumes

  • The institution's number is converted for you. Whichever way they quote it, the page finds the post-money after the whole seed and works from that. "iSAFE inside" means the angels' converted shares are part of their pre-money, which is the usual way. "iSAFE on top" means the pre-money covers only the existing holders and the angels sit on top of it, which is better for founders and worth asking for.
  • The cap is post-money by default. That is how the 100X.VC iSAFE is described by one law firm. Have your lawyer confirm the clause in the template you use, then switch the basis here if it is pre-money.
  • Angels convert at the best of three prices: the cap price, the discounted round price, or the round price itself. The calculator shows which one wins for each tranche.
  • The ceiling counts what founders give up. That is the angels plus the institution, and the pool top-up if the box is ticked. Before the seed, founders hold 90% and the pool 10% unless you change it.
  • Share counts are illustrative, on a base of 10,00,000 shares. Real counts depend on your actual cap table.
  • This is a planning tool. It is not legal or tax advice, and no Q1 2027 valuation is promised. Anyone who is an NRI or overseas needs the instrument checked with counsel.
