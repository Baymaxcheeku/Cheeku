Medicines

Over-the-counter list only: paracetamol, ORS, antacids, lozenges, simple antihistamines.

AntibioticsSleeping pillsCodeine syrupsSchedule H1 and XSick notes

The doctor decides

  • MBBS only, registration checked on the National Medical Register
  • Picks the diagnosis before any plan appears
  • Can edit, call the patient, decline or move to video

We audit the doctors

  • 10% of cases reviewed by a second doctor every week
  • Approval rate kept between 70% and 92%
  • Minimum review time; adverse events reviewed within 24 hours

The rules we follow

  • Telemedicine Practice Guidelines 2020: AI never prescribes
  • Consent on every case, records kept, prescription in the required format
  • A pharmacist checks before any medicine leaves

The case, as the system sees it

One state machine runs payments, promises and audits.

draft→eligible→paid→queued→claimed→approved→prescription issued→delivered or downloaded→check-in→closed
Side exits:not eligibleescalateddeclinedmoved to videorefundedexpired

Paid to claimed

10 min

90% of cases. Past 5 minutes the backup panel is alerted; past 10, the partner network takes it.

Claimed to decision

8 min

The case arrives prepared, so a review takes 3 to 5 minutes. About 12 an hour per doctor.

Paid to prescription

25 min

Past 30 minutes the money comes back automatically, with an apology.

₹99 a month
  • Unlimited chats, memory, health file, prescription checks
  • Care booked in chat; wearables connected
  • 2 doctor reviews a month included
  • Extra reviews ₹39
The reviews alone are worth ₹98. Two sick nights on passes cost the same as a month of Solo.
My family

Family

₹249 a month, up to 4
  • Everything in Solo, for four people, ₹62 each
  • Family memory and caregiver alerts for parents
  • 5 doctor reviews a month, shared, up to 2 a person
  • Extra reviews ₹39
Reviews worth ₹245 included. One parent's monthly fever and a child's... no: children go to video in v1, so the family's adults share the five.

Why 2 and 5, not 4 and 10

A review costs about ₹39 at scale. Four on Solo would cost ₹156 against ₹71 we keep after GST and store fees; ten on Family ₹390 against ₹179. Clinically, a third request for one person in 30 days should be a consult anyway.

House rules

  • A declined case or an ineligible one never uses an allowance
  • Third request for one person in 30 days goes to video
  • No rollover; the 10 conditions only
  • All payments in the app: UPI through Google Play and the App Store
