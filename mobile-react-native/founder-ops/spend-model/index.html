Assumptions

What this model assumes, and what it leaves out

Assumptions

  • Timing. Seed closes in Dec 2026; month 1 is Jan 2027, matching financial model v2.
  • Revenue. Monthly revenue and cost of revenue per scenario come straight from financial model v2 (subscriptions, bookings, employer seats, fitness). Inference cost per chat sits inside cost of revenue.
  • Salaries. Bengaluru and Gurugram startup ranges for 2026; founders at ₹24 lakh each. The salary lever scales every role.
  • Marketing. ₹8 to 12 lakh a month before launch, ₹38 to 42 lakh after launch, ₹45 lakh from month 13, split by channel as shown.
  • Model. Fine-tuning ₹3 to 5 lakh a month; a doctor-labelled 5,000-case eval set (₹21 lakh) plus refreshes; own GPUs ₹6 lakh a month from month 7 and ₹8 lakh from month 13; Hindi voice licensing from month 11.
  • Clinical safety and compliance. Licensed drug database ₹25 lakh a year; a paid doctor review panel growing from ₹3 to ₹8 lakh a month; a regulatory opinion and DPDP set-up (₹15 lakh); two security audits.

Left out, and why it matters

  • No Series A money. The plan stands on ₹26 crore alone. Financial model v2 also assumes ₹70 crore at month 13 to accelerate; this page shows the seed has to carry us there with buffer.
  • The full model spends faster. Financial model v2 hires about 50 people by month 18 and ramps marketing to ₹1.2 crore a month in 2028, which burns about ₹29 crore over 18 months. This plan holds 38 people and ₹45 lakh a month so ₹26 crore lasts.
  • No pendant, no state contracts, no own-model training run. Those are Series A decisions (see the Jivi teardown).
  • Taxes and GST input credit are not modelled; marketing and software include GST at cost.
  • Own GPUs are on by default because the deck promises them by the Series A. Turning them off saves about ₹1 crore and is what the Jivi teardown recommends until chat cost passes 15% of revenue.
