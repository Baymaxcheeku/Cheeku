Methodology

How the numbers are built

  • One model. This plan runs on the same month-by-month financial model behind our deck and projections; there is no separate spreadsheet to reconcile.
  • Seed only, or with the Series A. Our plan raises a Series A around month 13. "Seed only" shows how long ₹26 crore lasts if that round is delayed; "Seed + Series A" shows the full plan.
  • Defend mode. If runway falls below six months without new funding, we cut marketing by 65%, compute by 50% and hardware work by 80%, and freeze hiring. The toggle shows the effect.
  • Revenue comes from subscriptions, care bookings through partners, employer plans and fitness bookings, net of app store fees, payment costs and partner costs.
  • Salaries are team averages in 2027 rupees, rising 10% a year, plus 20% for benefits and statutory costs. Individual offers vary around the average within each team's budget.
