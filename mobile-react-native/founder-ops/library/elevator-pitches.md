# Elevator pitches (before and after)

1 Nov 2026. Shape of every pitch: who is hurting, what it costs them, what we do, one proof.

**Before:** "Cheeku is an AI-powered longitudinal health intelligence platform disrupting India's healthcare space."

## 1. The family health manager (default opener)
In most Indian homes, one son or daughter manages everyone's health from WhatsApp. Papa's reports sit with three labs, and nobody notices his sugar climbing for a year. Cheeku keeps the whole family's health in one place, spots the trend and books the test at home. Built with zero funding, live in 12,000 pincodes.

## 2. The two-minute consult
An Indian doctor gets about two minutes with each patient. The next 90 days, when health gets better or worse, families are on their own. Cheeku is with them every day in between: answers, reminders, and the next test booked on time. We launch in January.

## 3. The prescription nobody checked
A mother in Lucknow takes five medicines from three doctors. Nobody has checked whether they clash. Cheeku reads every prescription down to the salt and checks it against 10 published standards in seconds, then tells her what to ask her doctor.

## 4. Parents in another city
You live in Bengaluru. Your parents live in Patna. You find out about their health when something goes wrong. Cheeku tells you when Ma's blood pressure starts climbing and books the doctor before it becomes an emergency.

## 5. The money angle (commerce investors)
Indian families pay 39% of their health bills from their own pocket, much of it on tests they never look at again. Cheeku reads every report over time, so each test leads to the next right step. We earn when a family books that test or consult through us.

## 6. The deep tech angle (AI investors)
23 crore people a week ask ChatGPT health questions, and it forgets every one. Cheeku remembers: every report, prescription and reading, per family member. So the answer is about your father, not an average man. That memory is the data that trains India's own health model.

## 7. The employer angle (B2B2C investors)
Companies pay for health insurance that only works once an employee's family is in hospital. Cheeku keeps those families healthy before that, every day. Employers pay per seat, and their staff's families use it at home.

## How to use them
- Open with 1. Switch to 5, 6 or 7 for that investor type.
- Say one number, then stop. Let them ask.
- Never say: AI-powered, platform, disrupting, ecosystem, revolutionise.
- Rehearse each out loud under 20 seconds.

## 9. The healthspan pitch, two-minute version (Athar's structure)

In India, life expectancy is about 71 years. But healthy life expectancy is only about 60. That means the average Indian spends the last ten years of life dealing with illness: diabetes, blood pressure, heart and kidney problems, a parent who slowly stops being independent.

And those ten years don't arrive suddenly. They build up quietly for a decade before, in numbers nobody connects. A sugar reading that creeps up every year. A prescription from one doctor that clashes with another. A follow-up test that never got booked.

Over the last three decades, India has become good at the moment of care. You can find a doctor, book a lab test and get medicines delivered in a day. What nobody owns is everything around that moment: before the consultation, when you don't know whether something is serious or which doctor to see; after it, when you walk out with a prescription you can't read and a test list you don't understand; and the long stretch in between, when the daily choices are made alone, with Google and WhatsApp forwards. Every report sits with a different lab, every prescription with a different doctor, and no one holds the whole story.

That is the gap Cheeku fills. Cheeku is a 24/7 health companion for the whole family. It remembers every report, prescription and reading for each family member, so every answer is about your father, not an average man. It answers your health questions in plain language and cuts through the noise. When you need care, it books it right there: a home lab test, a doctor, a dietitian, across 12,000+ pincodes through our partners. And every day it helps you make small, personal choices, on food, medicines, sleep and the next test, that keep you healthier for longer.

Clinician-signed rules decide; the AI only explains, and it never diagnoses. Every answer and every booking adds to the family's health memory, so Cheeku gets more useful the longer you use it.

We have built the full product with zero funding, and we launch in January. Our goal is simple: give Indian families back those ten years.

(About 330 words, two minutes at a calm pace.)
