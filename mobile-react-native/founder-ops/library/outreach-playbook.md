## How to use this

Pick one narrative per investor, never two. Every narrative opens with its own pitch, shows the same traction and lands on the team, because at seed the team is the bet.

| Investor type | Narrative | Lead with | Best sender |
|---|---|---|---|
| AI and deep tech funds, AI founders | 1. Deep tech | The model, the evals, the data asset | Athar or Suraj |
| Seed generalist funds | 2. Seed | Built with ₹0, the gap, the team | Athar |
| Consumer funds (B2C) | 3. Consumer | Daily habit, families, free tier | Athar |
| Commerce and marketplace investors | 4. Commerce | Care booked in chat, take rate | Deepesh |
| B2B2C, fintech and insurtech investors | 5. B2B2C | Employers, insurers, the risk layer | Deepesh |
| Healthtech and longevity specialists | 6. Health | The 10 years spent unwell, clinical safety | Athar |
| Operator and founder angels | 7. Angels | Founders they would back, warm and short | Whoever knows them |

Rules for every message:

- **Make line one theirs.** Name a portfolio company, a post they wrote or a bet they made. The tracker's research and fit memo give you this.
- **One ask.** A 20-minute call, or an intro. Never both.
- **Numbers stay fixed.** Use only the figures in the next section. If a number changes, change it there first.
- **No fake scarcity.** Say the round is moving only when it is. Investors talk to each other.
- **Short wins.** Email under 150 words, WhatsApp under 60, LinkedIn connection note under 300 characters.
- **Log it.** Every message sent goes into the raise tracker with "Log as sent", so the follow-up date sets itself.

Placeholders are in curly brackets: {First name}, {Fund}, {Portfolio company}, {Specific reason}, {Mutual}.

## Narrative 8: Friends, family and close network

For people who know you personally: former colleagues, founder friends, family. This is your own voice, tidied up. It works because it reads like news shared with a friend, not a pitch. It feeds the ₹3 to 5 cr angel and friends-and-family part of the seed.

**What makes it work (keep these):**

- **It opens with you, not the company.** "Taking a big bet" and "all in" tell them this is personal, which is why a friend reads on.
- **One proof per co-founder, in plain words.** "Built a blast wave precision system for DRDO, aerospace engineer from IISc" lands harder than a list of titles.
- **The mission in one breath.** From before the consult to after it and beyond, so Indians live healthier and longer.
- **Hard numbers on supply.** Pincodes, cities, consultants, home delivery: proof that it is real, not a slide.
- **A soft ask.** "Let me know your feedback" plus the raise stated plainly. Friends invest when they are asked for their opinion first.
- **It feels first.** "Wanted to share first thing with a few close friends" is the FOMO, and it is honest.

**What to fix:** today it reads as if the 15 to 22B model exists, and the investor version says it ships in 2029. Say "building toward". Spell out "pincodes" and "parameters", and name Deepesh, because his Pine Labs record is your strongest proof on distribution.
