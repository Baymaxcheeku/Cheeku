The problem
India treats illness. Nobody manages health.
India's healthcare is vast and deeply fragmented. A family moves between clinics, labs, hospitals, pharmacies and specialists that never talk to each other. Their medical history lives in paper prescriptions, WhatsApp chats, hospital portals and someone's memory. The family is left to join the dots, and usually nobody does.
Fragmented care
Every visit starts from zero
Reports, prescriptions and scans sit in different places. Doctors can't see what another doctor did, so tests get repeated and medicines from three doctors are never checked together.
79.7 crABHA health IDs created, with records still in silos
Lost context
Two minutes with the doctor
Consults are short and specialists are scarce. Language barriers and low health literacy make reports and diagnoses hard to follow, so people leave with more questions than answers.
≈2 minaverage primary care consult in India
<1doctor for every 1,000 people
No continuous care
Chronic disease, managed alone
Diabetes, blood pressure and heart disease are decided in the months between visits: food, medicines, follow-ups. That stretch is left to Google and guesswork, and families pay the bill.
2 in 3deaths in India are from chronic disease
39.4%of health spending paid from families' own pockets
Our thesis
India has systems that treat individual illnesses. It has no companion that understands the person, remembers their history, connects their care and helps the family before, during and after every visit.
Proven markets
China has already proved it. India is next.
The first question is whether families will trust an app with their health. In China and the US they already do, at a scale few consumer products reach.

What we're building
The product
One health superapp to help families live longer and healthier.
Cheeku is a single app for a family's whole health life. It keeps one memory for everyone, from a newborn to a grandparent, and uses it every day to answer, check, plan and book. Chat is how people talk to it. The memory is what makes it work.

Before a visit
Ask anything in plain words and get an answer read against your own history. Know how urgent it is, and book the right test or doctor.
During
Walk in prepared with a one-page brief of symptoms, readings and medicines, and the questions worth asking.
After
Stay on track. Reports read and compared, prescriptions checked, medicines tracked, one daily step, and a reminder to retest.

Family health file
Built for Indian homes
Indian lab formats, medicine brands and Hinglish questions. One file for parents, children and in-laws, with alerts for whoever looks after them.
Safe by design
Code computes the numbers, clinicians set the rules, and the model explains in plain words. It never prescribes, and red flags go to a doctor.
Partners for care
Labs, doctors and pharmacies come through care partners. The conversation and the record stay with the family.
