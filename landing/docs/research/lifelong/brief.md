# Lifelong, studied 10 Nov 2026

Source: https://trylifelong.com, opened at 1440 and 390 wide with the
clone-website skill. The closest product to Cheeku the founder has found.
Studied for the hero and the navigation only. None of its text, photographs
or brand is in this project.

## Hero
- White page. The photograph is not full-bleed: it sits in a rounded card
  inset from the page edges, about one screen tall.
- Headline bottom left, two lines, the second part in a quieter tone. One
  sentence under it, then two pill buttons.
- Top right of the card: a small announcement chip.
- Bottom right: one question bubble and one answer card that names the
  document it read.
- Bright daylight photograph of two people at home.

## Navigation
- Plain white bar, wordmark left, three menus, one button right.
- "Product" opens a wide panel: two columns of features, each a soft
  coloured tile with an icon, a title and four or five words; a picture card
  on the right with one line and a link; a quiet row of links along the foot.

## What Cheeku took
- The inset photo card, the corner chip and the question-and-answer pair.
- The menu panel. Ours lists what Cheeku does and who it is for, and every
  item goes to a section of this page (there are no separate pages).

## What is Cheeku's own
- The line: "You look after everyone. Cheeku looks after it with you".
  Lifelong speaks to someone tracking one parent; Cheeku speaks to the person
  looking after the whole family.
- Ink text on the bright wall of the photograph, where Lifelong uses white
  text on a darkened photo. The founder asked for a light page throughout.
- The photograph: a young couple, their daughter and her grandmother.
- Serif display type, purple, marigold button.
