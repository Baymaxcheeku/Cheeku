# Octo Health, studied 10 Nov 2026

Source: https://octohealth.com/us/en, opened at 1440 and 390 wide. Studied
with the clone-website skill for structure, type, spacing and motion. None of
its text, photographs, product images or logo are in this project.

## What it is built from

| Section | How it works | What Cheeku took |
|---|---|---|
| Nav | Floating pill, clear over the hero, white after scrolling | Same |
| Hero | Full-screen photograph, headline bottom left, two pill buttons, a moving line of facts with a pause button | Same shape, our photo and words |
| How it works | Centred serif heading, three tall photo cards with glass panes of product detail | Same |
| Categories | Three rows of chips with round faces, sliding in opposite directions | The family, each with what Cheeku keeps for them |
| Outcomes | Six photo thumbnails switch one large photo panel with a headline, a chat exchange and a chart | Our six family situations, with a real app screen in place of the chart |
| Product | Tall cards that stack as you scroll, each sticky a little lower than the last | Same, with real app screens |
| Data wheel | Screen pins for two screens of scroll while a wheel of marker names turns past a soft glow | What Cheeku remembers, in purple |
| Comparison | Table with one filled column | Cheeku against Google and WhatsApp |
| Pricing | Three plans | Left out: no pricing while in stealth. Safety and questions sit here |
| Close | One line and a button | One line and the signup form |

## Measured

- Display: IBM Plex Serif 400, italic for emphasis. h1 68.8px / 1.05 / -0.015em. h2 46.4px.
- Body: Google Sans 400 and 500, 16 to 18px, line height 1.5 to 1.6.
- Page width 1180px, 24px gutters, sections 96px top and bottom.
- Buttons: full pills, 16px 32px in the hero, 10px 20px in the nav.
- Cards: 28 to 32px corners.
- Colour: cream #f7f4ee, ink #1c1b0f, forest green #173510, a lime button.

## What is Cheeku's own

- Purple and white in place of cream and green; marigold for the signup button.
- Instrument Serif and Inter, the two faces the Cheeku app already uses.
- Ten photographs of Indian families, generated for this page (public/photos).
- Real app screens in true iPhone frames.
- The angle: between doctor visits, for the whole family. Octo is a blood-test
  membership for one person.
